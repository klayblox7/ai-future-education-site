교육 방식 일러스트 프롬프트: "Create ONE clean 2 by 2 sprite sheet of four SEPARATE friendly 3D clay-style illustrations for a Korean AI education website. Use the attached screenshot ONLY as a style and subject reference. Each quadrant should contain exactly one isolated scene on a pure white background with generous blank margins. Top-left: smiling older Korean woman in violet sweater confidently learning AI on a laptop. Top-right: simple learning worksheet and a large pencil, symbolizing hands-on practice. Bottom-left: two soft blue chat bubbles with a clear question mark, symbolizing asking an instructor. Bottom-right: cheerful older Korean man with glasses using a laptop, with small travel and daily-life hints nearby. Four quadrants must be evenly spaced, visually consistent, and completely independent, with no figures crossing quadrant boundaries. Polished soft dimensional rendering, cobalt and sky-blue as the primary palette, small warm orange accents, approachable and easy to understand for all ages. NO letters, NO words, NO logos, NO numbers, NO borders, NO cards, NO connecting lines, NO watermark."
